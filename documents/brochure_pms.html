The Intelligence Turn

We live in an era when machines can draft a sentence in any language. What they cannot do is understand a civilization, read irony between lines written a century ago, or decide when not to translate. That human intelligence is exactly what this MA trains.

No written entrance exam. Selection is based on your portfolio (70%) and an oral interview (30%). We want to hear how you think.
